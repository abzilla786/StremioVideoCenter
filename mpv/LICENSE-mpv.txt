## Licence

The selected executable is a GPL-enabled MPV build, not the separately configured LGPL `libmpv` build. MPV upstream says the default program is GPLv2-or-later; its own licensing page explains that individual files and linked libraries can carry distinct licences: https://github.com/mpv-player/mpv/blob/master/Copyright. Existing build evidence indicates FFmpeg was configured with `--enable-gpl --enable-version3` and without `--enable-nonfree`, but those flags have not been independently re-extracted from the newly uploaded raw logs. Conservatively treat the executable stack as subject to GPL obligations until exact source/configuration is matched.

The standard licence texts are in `licenses/`. They do not replace the component-specific copyright notices and licence texts required for all components in the actual build.
